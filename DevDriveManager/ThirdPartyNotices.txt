AI Dev Gallery — NavigationView styles
https://github.com/microsoft/ai-dev-gallery
Source: AIDevGallery/Styles/NavigationView.xaml
Revision: 8e691a7e7af7159584b2642ce5eb738b5dbe8b3e

Themes/StoreNavigationView.xaml adapts the rail dimensions, item/presenter templates
and visual states from AI Dev Gallery. The platform NavigationView layout is retained;
Gallery-specific icon helpers, notification badges and Toolkit animations are omitted.
The adaptation uses explicit Light/Dark/HighContrast resources and system focus visuals.
